# Code of Conduct

ProjectVI is a community project. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, Discord or anywhere else the project lives, is expected to follow this code.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Help newcomers. Everyone started somewhere.

## What isn't accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Sharing other people's private information.
- Posting game files, keys, piracy links or cheats for other people's servers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content and warn, mute or ban people who break this code. Report problems privately to the maintainers. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is based on the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
